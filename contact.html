Frequently Asked Questions

How soon will you reply to my inquiry?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours on working days (Mon–Sat). Urgent queries may be directed to our phone line.

Can I visit your office for a consultation?

Yes! We welcome walk-in consultations during working hours. You can also book a slot in advance by calling or emailing us.

Do you offer services outside Kerala?

Yes, we operate pan-India. Events, digital marketing, admissions, and consultancy services are available nationwide.

What is the minimum budget for event management?

Our event packages start from ₹50,000 for smaller events. We'll customize a plan that fits your budget.

Are your courses available online?

Yes, select courses are available in online/hybrid format. Check each program's details on the Programs page for availability.

Do you provide placement after courses?

Yes, we offer career guidance and placement support after successfully completing our certified programs.
